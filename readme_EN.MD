## 🌱 Day one

Five clans of sapiens — twenty-five newborn minds, the first at the very centre of the island —,
herds of cows and sheep, wolves in packs, three solitary bears. Fire is not invented, writing
not dreamed of. **Everything else belongs to them.**

## 🏛️ The eras

| Era                  | What it brings                                      |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Neolithic            | fire → writing; round huts, the first camp          |
| Bronze Age           | plough, sail, science; stone houses                 |
| Iron Age             | philosophy, medicine; knowledge that no longer dies |
| Antiquity            | the City; paved squares, marble columns, roads      |
| Middle Ages          | universities, mills; walls and keeps                |
| Renaissance          | the printing press; painted facades                 |
| Industrial era       | steam, factories; smoking chimneys on the horizon   |
| Modern era           | electricity; night is no longer quite night         |
| **The Three Cities** | *written into the program — the people's future*    |

Passing an era is never automatic. When every technology of the age is found, when the people
is numerous and inventive enough, a **golden button** appears in the notebook — and waits for your hand.

## 👁️ See through their eyes

A hand-written 3D window — a **raycaster**, like the first games, in pure Pascal: grained
ground, distance fog, stone walls, thatch, fire flickering at night, factory smoke in the
industrial era. Three modes (right click):

1. **The stars** — every inhabitant is a point in speed × sight × size space: evolution at a glance;
2. **The populations** — the curves of history in relief;
3. **A sapien's eyes** — you see what HE sees, from his head: the forest, the ochre road winding ahead, the walls rising out of the mist.

And in this first-person world, **SPACE incarnates** the followed sapien: arrows or ZQSD to
walk, SPACE again hands the body back to its mind. Trades read at a glance — the shepherd's
staff, the hunter's spear, the fisher's rod — and the chief wears the diadem and the banner.

## 🏙️ The Observatory

- 🏘️ **Click a town** (lens tool): its record — rank, founding, hearths, inhabitants, roads, its chief's name;
- 🧠 **F7**: the specimen's brain rendered giant — a click toggles between the **weight graph** and the **mind mode**: what he perceives, his inner mood (tier 2, as living bars), what he wants, and the three heaviest reasons behind his decision;
- 📖 **F9**: the **people's dictionary** — every word struck, dated, signed;
- 📜 **The notebook**: annals, curves, the guessed lexicon.
