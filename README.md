✨ What emerges on its own · Ce qui émerge tout seul

    Evolution — natural selection on speed, sight, size, feather. Predatorspress, the prey lineage sharpens. · La sélection naturelle agit sur lestraits : vitesse, vue, taille, plume.
    Culture — each child picks a mentor; its mind glides toward theirs,but never beyond 30 % of the genetic distance. DNA is guided, neveroverwritten. · L'enfant choisit un mentor : son esprit glisse vers lesien, sans jamais écraser l'ADN.
    Language — four calls (α β γ δ) whose meaning is fixed by no one.The notebook guesses: alarm call? food? · Quatre cris dont le sensn'est fixé par personne — le carnet devine.
    Inventions — a skewer is born of a hungry night by the fire, a drumof a memory of danger by the water. Each takes its name from the emergentlexicon. · Les inventions naissent des circonstances, pas d'un plan.
    Cities — dense clusters of huts become named towns: paved squares,walls with four gates, a monument. They clear the forest around them,and roads slowly grow between them. · Les amas de huttes deviennent desbourgs nommés : place, remparts, monument — et des routes qui poussententre elles.
    Sound — everything synthesized live through waveOut: formant voices,drums, the lyre fanfare at each era's gate, crickets, swell, wind.· Tout l'audio est synthétisé en direct — voix à formants, lyre,cloches, grillons, houle.

🏛️ Six eras · Six ères
Era	What it brings
Neolithic	fire → writing; round huts, the first camp
Bronze Age	plough, sail, science; stone houses
Iron Age	philosophy, medicine; knowledge that no longer dies
Antiquity	the City; towns with paved squares, marble columns, roads
Middle Ages	universities, mills; walls and keeps
Renaissance	the printing press; painted facades

Passing an era is never automatic. When every technology of the age is found,when the people is numerous and inventive enough, a golden button appearsin the notebook — and waits for your hand.

Le passage d'ère n'est jamais automatique : quand tout est découvert, unbouton doré apparaît dans le carnet — et attend votre main.
🧠 The minds · Les esprits

    Not a trained model — a living species.Pas un modèle entraîné — une espèce.

Every sapien carries its own neural network — 34 → 9 → 9 → 10, about 600weights of mental DNA. Three slow reflections and a direct reflex path,nine echoes of its own last decisions. No backpropagation, no reward, nogoal: inheritance, mentorship and selection shape everything.

Chaque sapiens porte son propre réseau neuronal — hérité, muté, sélectionnépar la seule survie. Trois forces le façonnent : l'héritage, le mentorat,la sélection.
